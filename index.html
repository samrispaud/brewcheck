About BrewCheck

BrewCheck checks user queries against a database of community-tested gluten test results for 170 beers. It runs entirely in your browser — no accounts, no analytics, no network calls beyond fetching the dataset.

Personal use only — not medical advice

The information here is gathered from public test results and is not a substitute for guidance from a doctor or registered dietitian. If you have celiac disease, when in doubt, avoid the beer.

About the FDA standard

The FDA defines "gluten-free" as containing fewer than 20 ppm of gluten. BrewCheck color-codes beers based on test results relative to that threshold and consistency across batches.

Batch variation

Beers marked Batch Variation have shown inconsistent results across different batches — some test safe, others don't. We do not recommend these beers for celiac diets.
